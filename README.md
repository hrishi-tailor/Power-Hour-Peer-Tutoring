# [📚 Power Hour Peer Tutoring Website](https://powerhourpeertutoring.vercel.app/)  

An elegant and intuitive landing page designed to revolutionize the way tutors and tutees connect, streamlining peer tutoring coordination through innovative use of web technologies and seamless data management.

---

## 🌟 **Project Overview**  
As the President of **Power Hour Peer Tutoring**, I developed this website to support my school’s peer tutoring initiative and make life easier for both tutors and tutees. This project was built independently, out of the kindness of my heart, for a teacher who didn’t request it, but I recognized the need for a more organized and efficient system.  

Built with **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript**, the platform serves as a digital gateway for connecting learners with volunteer tutors, fostering education and skill-building in a way that benefits the entire school community.

---

### **Key Features**  
- **Responsive Design**: The website is fully responsive, providing an optimal viewing experience across devices.  
- **Google Form Integration**: Seamlessly redirects user-submitted information to a Google Form for data collection.  
- **Automated Data Organization**: Collected data is transferred to an Excel spreadsheet, enabling quick analysis and pairing of tutors with tutees.  
- **Enhanced Accessibility**: Tutors earn valuable volunteer hours, and tutees receive the personalized academic support they need.

---

## 🔧 **Technical Highlights**  
- **Frontend Technologies**:  
  - Designed using **HTML** for structure, **CSS** for styling, and **JavaScript** for interactivity.  
  - Focused on aesthetic simplicity and intuitive navigation for all users.  
- **Data Workflow**:  
  - Integrated with Google Forms for dynamic form submission.  
  - Leveraged **Google Sheets** (Excel) for efficient data organization, sorting, and pairing logic.  
- **Scalability**:  
  - The modular codebase allows easy adaptation for future enhancements and integration with more advanced data management tools.

---

## 🎯 **Impact and Benefits**  
- **For Tutors**:  
  - Simplifies the process of finding tutoring opportunities, ensuring accurate logging of volunteer hours.  
- **For Tutees**:  
  - Provides an easy-to-use platform to request help in their desired subjects, fostering academic growth.  
- **For Administrators**:  
  - Saves time by automating the matching process through structured data collection and Excel-based workflows.  

---

## 🛠️ **How It Works**  
1. **User Interaction**:  
   - Tutees and tutors input their details into the website's form.  
2. **Form Submission**:  
   - Data is sent to a connected Google Form.  
3. **Data Management**:  
   - Information is auto-organized in an Excel (Google Sheets) spreadsheet for easy tutor-tutee pairing.
